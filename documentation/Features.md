# FitMentor AI — Core Features

## 1. Executive Dashboard
- **Daily Readiness & Health Score**: Radial gauge showing current readiness (0–100) combining recovery index, sleep quality score, daily activity load, and resting heart rate.
- **Top Metrics Strip**: Live step tracker with goal percentage, active calorie burn sparkline, sleep cycle bar visualization, and resting heart rate monitor.
- **Active Workout Widget**: Quick-resume training session with exercise count, estimated duration, completion percentage, and category tag.
- **AI Daily Briefing**: Personalized text insights highlighting readiness level, suggested emphasis, and safety tips.
- **Weekly Consistency Chart**: Interactive bar chart comparing daily workout completion and volume load across Monday–Sunday.
- **Goal & Milestone Progress**: Target program tracker (e.g. 12-Week Hypertrophy) with milestone badges and unlocks.

## 2. Smart Workout Engine
- **Periodized Routines**: Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower, Full Body, and HIIT splits tailored to user goal (Hypertrophy, Fat Loss, Strength, Endurance).
- **Interactive Workout Mode**:
  - Live session timer with exercise step-by-step checklist.
  - Rep and weight logger with RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) inputs.
  - Auto-countdown rest timer with audio/visual notification.
- **Computer Vision Form Checker**: Camera analysis estimating joint angles (elbow flare, spinal alignment, hip hinge depth).

## 3. Dynamic Nutrition & Macro Planner
- **Target Macro Rings**: Real-time tracking of Protein, Carbohydrates, Healthy Fats, and Total Calories.
- **Smart Meal Recommender**: AI-generated meal ideas tailored to remaining macros for the day and dietary preferences (High Protein, Keto, Vegan, Mediterranean).
- **Hydration Tracker**: Daily water intake counter with quick add buttons (+250ml, +500ml).

## 4. Conversational AI Coach
- **Natural Language Chat**: Multi-turn dialogue for fitness advice, alternative exercise recommendations, deload scheduling, and recovery optimization.
- **Pre-set Smart Prompts**: Quick 1-click prompts ("Analyze my recovery", "Suggest a quick 20m workout", "How much protein for dinner?").
- **Voice / Audio Response Ready**: Structured output compatible with Web Speech API.

## 5. Wearables & Telemetry Integration
- **Smartwatch Sync Status**: Real-time connection badge (WearOS, Galaxy Watch, Apple Watch) showing last sync timestamp and battery status.
- **Live Biometric Telemetry**: Heart rate zones (Zone 1 Recovery to Zone 5 Peak) and HRV (Heart Rate Variability) analysis.
