# FitMentor AI — Problem Statement

## Background & Context

In the modern fitness landscape, individuals face major challenges in achieving their health and wellness goals:

1. **High Cost & Inaccessibility of Personal Trainers**: Quality 1-on-1 personal coaching often costs $60–$150+ per hour, making expert human coaching prohibitively expensive for most people.
2. **Lack of Dynamic Progression & Personalization**: Traditional fitness apps rely on static, generic PDF-like routines that fail to adapt when users miss workouts, experience excessive fatigue, suffer minor injuries, or plateau.
3. **Improper Exercise Form & Injury Risk**: Beginners and intermediate athletes frequently execute compound lifts (squats, deadlifts, bench presses) with poor biomechanics, leading to acute strain or chronic injuries without immediate form feedback.
4. **Disjointed Ecosystem & Data Silos**: Nutrition logging, workout tracking, sleep monitors, and smartwatch wearables exist in fragmented applications, preventing users from seeing a unified picture of their systemic recovery and readiness.

## The Solution: FitMentor AI

**FitMentor AI** bridges these gaps by providing an **AI-powered personal fitness coaching ecosystem**:
- **Continuous Readiness & Recovery Monitoring**: Computes a dynamic daily Readiness Score (0–100) factoring in sleep duration, resting heart rate, active calories, and muscular recovery load.
- **Adaptive Intelligent Programming**: Generates and dynamically adjusts workout intensity, volume, and rest intervals based on real-time fatigue and biometrics.
- **Interactive AI Coach**: Always-accessible conversational AI assistant providing nutritional advice, technique cues, and workout adjustments in natural language.
- **Computer Vision Form Correction**: Real-time pose estimation and kinematic angle analysis via smartphone camera or webcam to identify lumbar rounding, knee valgus, or suboptimal range of motion.
- **Connected Smartwatch Telemetry**: Seamless synchronization with WearOS/Galaxy Watch and Apple Watch for real-time heart rate zones, active calories, and step tracking.
